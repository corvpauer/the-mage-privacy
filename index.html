Privacy Policy

The Mage - Tower Defense · Last updated September 25, 2026

The game is playable without watching ads. Rewarded ads are optional and grant Diamonds only after a completed ad.

Who operates the game

The Mage - Tower Defense is published by C&P DEV. For privacy questions or requests, contact corvpauerhd@gmail.com.

Game progress

The game does not require an account. It stores progress, currencies, cards, upgrades, and research timers on your device so you can continue playing. C&P DEV does not operate a game account or cloud-save server for this version. You can remove locally stored game progress by clearing the app's storage or uninstalling it; device backups may retain a copy according to your device settings.

Privacy choices and optional ads

On Android, the app uses Google's User Messaging Platform (UMP) to request and remember privacy choices where required. The Cards screen provides a Privacy button when privacy options are available. You can use it to review or change your choices. The game loads rewarded ads only when the consent flow permits ad requests; an ad is shown only if you choose to watch it.

To provide and measure ads, detect fraud, and report performance, advertising providers may process technical information such as your IP address, approximate location inferred from it, device and operating-system information, advertising ID when available, consent choices, and ad interactions. C&P DEV can see advertising performance reports from its providers. The providers' own notices explain their processing and retention in more detail.

Providers

  • Unity provides LevelPlay mediation and Unity Ads, including related ironSource technology.
  • Google provides UMP consent messaging. This version uses an AdMob app ID for that message; it does not use an AdMob ad unit to show ads.
  • GitHub Pages hosts this privacy-policy page. GitHub may process technical information when you visit it.

The consent screen lists the advertising partners included in the current ad setup. We will update this policy if we add providers or materially change data use.

Retention and your choices

Local game progress remains until you clear the app's storage or remove the app, subject to device backups. Consent choices are stored by UMP and can be revisited through the in-game Privacy button when available. Advertising providers keep information according to their own policies. You can also reset or delete your Android advertising ID in your device settings. Contact us at the address above with a privacy request.

Changes

We may update this policy when the game or its providers change. The date at the top shows the latest revision.
